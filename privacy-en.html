Privacy policy

Effective 20 November 2026. App: CarCleanzer (com.carcleanzer.app). Developer: Meteoric Potential Unipessoal Lda (Dayava Corporation), dayavacorp@gmail.com.

In short

CarCleanzer does not collect, store on servers or share your personal data with third parties. There are no ads, analytics or trackers in the app. Everything happens between your phone and your head unit.

What the app does

  • It looks for an Android car head unit on your local network (Wi-Fi or your phone's hotspot) and connects to it over ADB, the same way a computer does. With your consent it runs commands on the head unit: reads device information and the list of apps, turns off the apps you select, clears the cache, changes animation speed, turns on private DNS — only what you ticked.
  • The key used to connect to the head unit and the change log (for undo) are stored only inside the app on your phone and are deleted together with the app.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state — only to find the head unit and connect to it on the local network. The app does not send your data to servers on the internet. Links (the author's channel, other apps) open only when you tap them.

Report

The check report (head unit model, Android version, list of changes) is created on the phone. It leaves the phone only if you tap «Copy report» and paste it somewhere yourself. If you send it to us, we use it only to answer your question.

Purchases

If paid features appear in the app, payments go through Google Play or the App Store; they process the payment details, we never receive them.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect children's data.
