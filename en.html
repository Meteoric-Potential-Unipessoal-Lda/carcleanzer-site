A slow head unit —
fast again, from your phone

CarCleanzer connects to an Android car head unit over ADB (like a technician's computer), checks it and shows what slows it down. It turns off the junk in batches and after each batch checks the home screen, Settings and the unit's own services (MCU, CAN, Bluetooth). Any change can be undone in the History tab. No root, no laptop.

This is a beta. Head units differ a lot: FYT, Teyes, Topway TS, MTC/PX5, Reglink, Allwinner and others. If something goes wrong, tap «Copy report» and send it to us. Without a car you can try the demo head unit inside the app.

How to connect

  1. Turn on the hotspot on your phone and connect the head unit to it.
  2. Head unit: Settings → «About device» → tap «Build number» 7 times → «Developer options» → «USB debugging».
  3. If there is «ADB over network» / «Network debugging», turn it on. On Android 11+ you can use «Wireless debugging» with a pairing code.
  4. In CarCleanzer tap the found head unit → «Check the head unit» (read-only) → tick what you need → «Apply».
